Here are the total sales by month for this year. I’ve generated a chart and key insights for you.
▤  Generated SQL
SELECT
  DATE_TRUNC('month', order_date) AS month,
  SUM(total_amount) AS total_sales
FROM sales
WHERE order_date >= '2026-01-01'
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month;
